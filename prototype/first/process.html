From shell to finished briquette

A considered process, step by step.

Each stage transforms selected coconut shells into briquettes prepared for customers and export.

Our Production Journey

Five stages. One consistent focus.

Production moves from raw material preparation through shaping, drying, and final packing.

  1. Raw material

    Shell selection

    Coconut shells are selected and prepared as the raw material for charcoal production.

  2. Conversion

    Carbonization

    Prepared shells are processed into coconut charcoal for the briquette mixture.

  3. Preparation

    Grinding and mixing

    Charcoal is ground to controlled particle sizes, then mixed in preparation for pressing.

  4. Shaping

    Pressing

    The prepared charcoal mixture is shaped into the selected briquette format.

  5. Preparation to ship

    Drying and packaging

    Briquettes are dried, reviewed, and packed for shipment according to the agreed order requirements.
