Our Process

From shell to premium charcoal.

A carefully managed production journey designed to maintain consistency from raw material to final packaging.

Carbonization

Grinding

Charcoal is processed into controlled particle sizes.

Pressing

Charcoal powder is shaped into uniform briquettes.

Packaging

Final products are packed and prepared for export.
